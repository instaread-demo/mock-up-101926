Don’t look now, but we’re already in Week 7 of the 2026 college football season. Time flies when you’re having fun, and we’re set to have a tremendous time today, thanks to the SEC’s incredible slate.

College football’s premier conference boasts three ranked-on-ranked matchups this afternoon, including a Missouri-Ole Miss clash that includes two top 13 teams. We also get a pair of games between currently ranked and once-top 10 teams (Kentucky-Oklahoma, Florida-Texas) that are hoping to save their seasons. And, last but not least, some intense rivalries — The Third Saturday of October and The Deep South’s Oldest Rivalry — add to their legacies.

Autumn started a month ago, but the weather is finally starting to feel like it. As the temperature drops, the stakes for College Football Playoff runs only rise. Here’s the SEC’s full Week 7 schedule, featuring 15 of the conference’s 16 teams (South Carolina is on bye):

  • Florida vs. No. 7 Texas — 12:00 p.m. ET (ABC)
  • No. 16 Mississippi State vs. No. 22 LSU — 12:00 p.m. ET (ESPN)
  • The Citadel at Texas A&M — 1:00 p.m. ET (SEC Network+)
  • Auburn vs. No. 1 Georgia — 3:30 p.m. ET (ESPN)
  • No. 6 Alabama vs. No. 14 Tennessee — 3:30 p.m. ET (ABC)
  • No. 13 Missouri at No. 9 Ole Miss — 3:30 p.m. ET (SEC Network)
  • Arkansas vs. Vanderbilt — 7:00 p.m. ET (ESPN2)
  • No. 15 Kentucky vs. Oklahoma — 7:30 p.m. ET (SEC Network)

Enjoy the games, everyone!
